Apple TV has no web browser, so EYE3 reaches the big screen through AirPlay: mirror it from your iPhone, iPad or Mac. It is free, built in, and needs no app.

  1. 1
    Join the same Wi-Fi

    Put your iPhone, iPad or Mac and your Apple TV on the same network.

  2. 2
    Open EYE3 on your device

    Scan the code with your iPhone camera, or copy the link to your Mac.

    Loading code…

  3. 3
    Mirror to your Apple TV

    iPhone or iPad: open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring, choose your Apple TV. Mac: menu bar Control Center, Screen Mirroring, choose your Apple TV.

    Presentation hides the menus, switches to the large TV layout and keeps the screen awake. Turn your phone sideways to fill the TV.
