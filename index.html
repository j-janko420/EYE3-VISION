Put EYE3 on your TV. Pick the route your TV and phone support.

iPhone with a TV that has no browser or AirPlay (for example Philips Saphi)

  1. A
    HDMI cable (most reliable, no Wi-Fi needed)

    Connect the iPhone to the TV's HDMI port with a USB-C to HDMI adapter (iPhone 15 and later) or Apple's Lightning Digital AV Adapter (older iPhones). Pick that HDMI input on the TV, open EYE3 on the iPhone and press Start presentation below. Turn the phone sideways to fill the screen.

  2. B
    Streaming stick

    Plug an Apple TV into the TV and use AirPlay (below). Or plug in a Fire TV or Google TV stick and open EYE3 in its browser. TV mode and the built-in casting then work.

  3. C
    Mirroring app

    Some iPhone apps mirror to Philips TVs over Wi-Fi (DLNA). Quality varies and most are paid. IPTV player apps can only play video channels, so they can't run EYE3.

Built-in casting to a TV browser, tablet or computer

For screens that have a web browser. Both devices on the same Wi-Fi. EYE3 runs on the screen, and your iPhone sends its image or live camera to it.

1. On the TV: show a pairing code

Open EYE3 in the screen's browser (for example a Fire TV or Google TV stick, a smart TV with a browser, or a laptop connected to the TV), go to Connect TV and press the button.

2. On the iPhone: scan or type the code

Scan the TV's code with the iPhone camera app, or scan it here. Then pick an image or start the camera, and it appears on the TV with its detections. The picture goes straight to the TV over your Wi-Fi and never leaves your network.

Or mirror the whole screen (Miracast or AirPlay)

  1. 1
    Join the same Wi-Fi

    Put your phone or computer and your TV on the same network.

  2. 2
    Open EYE3 on your device

    Scan the code with your phone camera, or copy the link to your computer. TV with a web browser? Open the same link on the TV itself. TV mode turns on automatically, or add ?tv=1 to the link.

    Loading code…

  3. 3
    Mirror to your TV

    Miracast TVs (many Philips Saphi models): on the TV, turn on Screen Mirroring from the Home menu or Sources (the name varies by model). Then on an Android phone open Quick Settings and tap Cast, Smart View or Screen Cast, or on a Windows PC press Win + K, and choose your TV.

    Apple TV and AirPlay TVs: on an iPhone or iPad open Control Center and tap Screen Mirroring; on a Mac use Control Center in the menu bar.

    iPhones can't use Miracast. With a Miracast-only TV such as Philips Saphi, use an HDMI adapter or a streaming stick (see above).

    Presentation hides the menus, switches to the large TV layout and keeps the screen awake. Turn your phone sideways to fill the TV.
